Extended repository search. Rule in extended_data_search_rule.txt. No API key for Synapse, OSF, GitHub, Zenodo, Figshare, or CELLxGENE was present in the environment. Public endpoints were queried. No new expression matrix was opened.

Primary-tumor brain relapse
- GSE26338 sample headers, seven platforms. Characteristics record relapse-free survival, not the organ of relapse. GPL5325 has 9 samples labeled tumor: brain metastasis. That is the metastasis, and 9 is not more than 23 primary-tumor events. Expression was not downloaded.

Procedure-matched metastatic sites
- Synapse public search returned project descriptions and a copy of GSE12276. It did not return a public count matrix with two metastatic sites collected by one procedure.
- OSF SHARE returned gene-level notes about central nervous system metastasis, not a cohort.
- Zenodo keyword search did not return a public patient cohort meeting the rule. The PEACE melanoma autopsy deposit is bulk DNA and RNA from an autopsy programme. Bulk brain expression is confounded by glia, and the single-cell data in that paper are whole-genome, not a macrophage-resolved transcriptome. Expression was not downloaded.
- CELLxGENE, 2,295 datasets. The colorectal metastasis atlas has liver, lung, and peritoneum and does not have brain or lymph node as a required side. The HTAN MSK small-cell atlas is annotated with adrenal, axilla, bone, brain, liver, lung, lymph node, and pleural effusion, but the Cancer Cell paper states the distant sites as liver, adrenal, axilla, and pleural effusion, and states that specimens came from surgical resection or tissue biopsy. Mixed procedures fail the rule. Counts were not downloaded.
- GSE162102, autopsy bulk: 4 lymph-node metastases, 2 primary tumors, 1 liver metastasis, 1 intrapulmonary metastasis, 4 normal lung. No brain sample, and the liver side has one sample.
- GSE280739 is a mouse xenograft and circulating-cell experiment, not paired human metastatic organs.
- GSE216069 is a fresh-versus-frozen methods series. Brain samples are non-small-cell lung cancer or melanoma metastases. Liver samples are uveal melanoma. The two organs are not two sites of one cancer collected by one procedure.
- GSE185386 remains the melanoma atlas already rejected because brain samples were surgical and extracranial samples were biopsies.

No series qualified. The locked gene lists were not scored.

Authenticated Synapse search, second pass. All 448 projects returned for brain and metastasis were paged. Forty-three also mention a site, relapse, biopsy, autopsy, or a single-cell assay; their top-level file names were listed. syn57077557 contains 76 breast and 55 lung neuroimaging files (NIfTI and AFNI BRIK/HEAD) that map the anatomic distribution of metastases inside the brain. Those files were listed and then removed locally. They are not expression and they have no per-tumor relapse label. syn34512705 is normal human leptomeninges. syn22364283 is targeted-sequencing VCF files. syn73580025 is bone metastasis only. syn1688370 names an OsloVal clinical table; the download returned HTTP 403, so the table was not read. GENIE and GLASS, checked with the same token. AACR Project GENIE public release 20, data_clinical_sample.txt, 289,873 samples. Sample type is primary, metastasis, distant organ, or lymph node. The organ is not named, and no sample has brain in the site field. The assay is a targeted DNA panel, copy number, and structural variants. There is no transcriptome, so the locked genes cannot be scored.

GENIE BPC breast public release 1.0, cancer-level table, 1,141 records. The field dist_mets_brain_cns is 1 for 362 records and 0 for 768. GENIE BPC NSCLC public release 2.0 also records brain as a distant site; Brain NOS is a common first distant site. Both releases are panel DNA plus curated clinical fields. They do not contain expression of the locked genes. GLASS, syn17038081, is the Glioma Longitudinal Analysis Consortium. Its sample tables are glioma specimens and glioma RNA. The tumors are already in the brain, so the cohort does not compare organs. These three datasets were not used to score the locked genes.

Broader Synapse pass. Projects, files, folders, tables, and datasets were searched under separate terms (AURORA, Hartwig, TRACERx, MBCproject, HTAN, leptomeningeal, organotropism, autopsy, PEACE, MET500, CNS, single-cell, relapse, and the clinical-table and dataset indexes). Hits were then resolved by name. The seven projects matching AURORA are aurora-kinase or unrelated studies. Hartwig, TRACERx, MBCproject, MET500, and Brastianos returned no project. The Hartwig-named folder inside the Sottoriva project is neoantigen annotation of TCGA mutation calls. The nine public HTAN projects are demos, image tools, a publication-text extract, colorectal spatial images, and liver-senescence labels. syn20446927 and syn22123910 returned HTTP 403. Leptomeningeal hits other than the normal-meninges study are BraTS challenges and an Alzheimer challenge. Organotropism hits are the pancreatic liver-versus-lung project already set aside and the IACM center, whose files are progress reports and meeting recordings. The 14 metastasis clinical tables are neurofibromatosis, BeatAML, or a breast single-cell table with no organ column. Row queries on the tables that do have a tissue or organ column returned HTTP 403, except a deprecated catalog whose readable rows are SNP-array dosage files with a blank organ field. Dataset hits for brain are Alzheimer, psychiatric, and neurofibromatosis atlases. BraTS-MET is an MRI challenge. No expression matrix was opened, and no series met the eligibility rule.
