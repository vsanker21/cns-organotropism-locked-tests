Locked claim. No new gene is chosen. The three Bos genes had been frozen and left unscored by a 15-gene minimum; that set is scored once below.

These programs do not establish a transferable organotropism mechanism. A culture-derived malignant program, a published neural program, and the three-gene Bos mediator set do not predict brain relapse in the external primary-tumor cohort. A leptomeningeal-versus-parenchymal program that agrees across five mouse models does not mark human malignant cells in the predicted direction, and inside one human study it does not separate pleural effusion, brain, and lymph node. Recruited macrophages in that study differ between neurosurgical brain metastases and bronchoscopic lymph-node metastases after ambient correction. The 216 genes from that contrast do not separate surgically resected brain metastases from bone and adrenal metastases. No other public single-cell series can hold the collection procedure constant and still compare two metastatic sites.

The intervals and sample sizes below are part of the claim. A non-significant test is not a demonstration that the effect is zero. No larger public primary-tumor series with a brain-relapse label was available, so those intervals were not recomputed.

1. Primary tumors. Step A is 253 genes from GSE138122, brain-tropic versus lung-tropic, absolute log2 fold change greater than 1 and FDR below 0.05. The neural set is Gavish malignant metaprogram 29, NPC/OPC, 50 genes. In GSE76714, 23 tumors with a brain metastasis and 48 without, the Step A odds ratio per standard deviation is 1.15 (0.70 to 1.88), AUC 0.55, logistic p 0.59. The neural odds ratio is 0.93 (0.57 to 1.54), AUC 0.44, p 0.79. In GSE12276, 16 brain-relapse cases among 185 labeled tumors, the Step A odds ratio is 1.38 (0.81 to 2.37), AUC 0.58, p 0.23. The neural odds ratio is 1.06 (0.63 to 1.77), AUC 0.51, p 0.84. Both intervals include an odds ratio near 2. The scoring pipeline recovers the Smid brain-up set on GSE12276, the cohort from which it was derived: odds ratio 1.81 (1.09 to 3.01), AUC 0.69, p 0.021. On GSE76714 that same set is an odds ratio of 1.36 (0.81 to 2.29), p 0.25. The frozen Bos set is PTGS2, HBEGF, and ST6GALNAC5. A 15-gene minimum had left this complete set unscored. Scored as the mean of the three gene-wise z-scores, with every gene present in both cohorts, the external result in GSE76714 is an odds ratio of 0.70 (0.42 to 1.15), AUC 0.40, logistic p 0.16. The mean score is lower in the tumors that developed a brain metastasis. GSE12276 gives an odds ratio of 1.68 (1.01 to 2.80), logistic p 0.045, and is not independent evidence, because these genes are tied to that series.

The Step A and neural intervals include an odds ratio near 2 because the brain-relapse counts are 23 and 16. Before any further expression was scored, sample metadata were counted for an independent primary-tumor series. GSE2034 has a brain-relapse column: 10 events among 286 tumors. That count is smaller than the cohorts already tested, so those tumors were not scored. GSE5327, GSE20685, GSE58812, GSE11121, and GSE7390 record distant relapse or death and do not record brain relapse. GSE2603 records lung-metastasis and a paired bm event in a lung-metastasis series; that field is not a brain-relapse label. The public clinical files for METABRIC and SCAN-B, as used in the studies that report them, contain survival and not the organ of relapse. A later metadata search, run before any new expression download, found the same limit. The open TCGA-BRCA clinical follow-up tables name the brain for 8 patients. GSE345865 labels 23 primary breast-cancer samples as brain-metastasis positive and 19 as negative, which is not a larger event count than GSE76714. GSE248830 is paired primary tumor and brain metastasis, GSE281580 is five brain metastases and three primary tumors, and GSE322943 is breast-cancer brain metastasis only. None of those series meets the rule for a larger relapse cohort or for two metastatic sites taken by the same procedure, so their expression matrices were not downloaded. A further search of Synapse, OSF, Zenodo, GitHub, CELLxGENE, and GEO, with no repository API key present in the environment, did not find a qualifying series. GSE26338 records relapse but not brain as the site, apart from 9 samples that are brain metastases. The HTAN small-cell atlas lists liver, adrenal, lymph node, axilla, and effusion, and its methods mix resection with biopsy. GSE162102 is autopsy bulk RNA with one liver metastasis and no brain sample. GSE216069 compares fresh and frozen brain or liver samples from different cancers. PEACE autopsy RNA-seq is bulk, and bulk brain remains confounded by glia. An authenticated Synapse search paged all 448 projects indexed under brain and metastasis. Forty-three of those also mention a metastatic site, relapse, or a single-cell assay, and their top-level files were listed. The one project named as a brain-metastasis study, syn57077557, contains MRI distribution maps of where breast and lung metastases sit in the brain, in NIfTI and AFNI format, not a gene-expression matrix and not a relapse table. The human leptomeninges project is normal meninges. The metastatic-breast targeted-sequencing project is variant files without a site table. The bone-metastasis project is bone only. The OsloVal clinical table is present and returned HTTP 403, so it was not read. AACR Project GENIE release 20 does not name the metastatic organ. GENIE BPC breast flags brain or central nervous system distant metastasis for 362 of 1,141 records, and the non-small-cell lung release records brain as a distant site. Both are targeted DNA panels, with no expression of the locked genes. GLASS is longitudinal glioma, already in the brain. None of these was scored. The wide intervals stay in the claim.

2. Compartment. The mouse rule kept a gene only when the leptomeningeal-versus-parenchymal sign agreed in at least four of five models and the inverse-variance meta-analysis FDR was below 0.05. That rule produced 805 leptomeningeal genes and 464 parenchymal genes. Dropping any one model leaves 803 to 805 of the 805 leptomeningeal genes positive. Human orthologs used for scoring were 711 and 406. In malignant cells, six cerebrospinal-fluid samples from GSE276139 against ten brain-parenchyma samples from GSE131907, the leptomeningeal score is lower in fluid than in parenchyma (means −0.14 versus +0.08, two-sided p 0.15). The parenchymal score is higher in every fluid sample than in every parenchyma sample (means +0.57 versus −0.34, p 0.00025), which is the reverse of the mouse prediction. The G2M score is also higher in fluid (means +0.47 versus −0.28, p 0.0075), and 83 percent of its genes are higher in fluid. The reverse parenchymal difference is therefore not a compartment result. It comes from two studies and moves with proliferation. Inside GSE131907 alone, two of five effusion samples, ten brain samples, and seven lymph-node samples had at least 20 malignant cells. All six pre-specified contrasts have an FDR of 0.66. The fluid-versus-solid reading and the brain-versus-node reading are not supported. The effusion contrast has two samples.

3. Macrophages. In GSE131907 the comparison is monocyte-derived macrophages, ten neurosurgical brain metastases against seven bronchoscopic lymph-node metastases, with no patient in both groups. Before ambient correction, 280 genes have FDR below 0.05, 171 higher in brain and 109 higher in node, and the sample-label permutation p is 0.013. A sex-adjusted model retains 278 of those calls, and a floor of 50 macrophages retains 303. The pre-specified microglia markers do not meet FDR below 0.05 and log2 fold change greater than 1. After DecontX correction within each sample, the permutation p is 0.0076 and 216 genes remain at FDR below 0.05, 120 higher in brain and 96 higher in node. One hundred ninety-two of the original 280 remain. GLDN and SLC1A3 remain higher in brain. This is a difference between those two sample groups. Site and procedure are the same variable, so it is not a brain-versus-other-site result. In GSE123902, three brain, one bone, and one adrenal metastasis, all surgical and all with at least 20 macrophages, the brain-high score has means −0.038 in brain and +0.057 in bone plus adrenal (p 0.80) and the ranges overlap. The lymph-node-high score has means −0.025 and +0.038 (p 1.00) and the ranges overlap. Removing GLDN, SLC1A3, GFAP, PLP1, CLDN11, APOD, MBP, MOG, OLIG1, OLIG2, AQP4, and S100B leaves the same overlap. With three samples against two, complete separation would still have a two-sided Mann–Whitney p of 0.20. The samples do not separate, so the 216 genes are retired. The metadata search found no other public single-cell series with two metastatic sites collected by the same procedure and at least two samples on each side.

Nothing in these tests is a new organotropism mechanism. No further gene list is chosen from GSE138122, GSE221358, GSE131907, GSE123902, or the series examined in the procedure search.
